🚀 GETTING STARTED

Welcome to Tactical Games

This is a comprehensive tactical gaming platform featuring 9 different games, achievement tracking, progression system, and real-time leaderboards.

First Steps

  1. Play Games - Visit the home page and select any game to start playing
  2. Earn XP - Every game you complete grants Experience Points
  3. Level Up - Accumulate 1000 XP to level up
  4. Unlock Achievements - Complete special challenges to unlock badges
  5. Compete - Check the leaderboard to see how you rank against others
Make sure to keep playing consistently to unlock more achievements and level up faster!

🎮 GAME GUIDES

TIC-TAC-TOE

Objective: Get three marks in a row (horizontal, vertical, or diagonal)
How to Play: Click a cell to place your mark (X). The AI plays as (O). Win by getting 3 in a row before the AI does.
Strategy: Control the center, corner placement matters. Try to create multiple winning paths.

MEMORY

Objective: Match all pairs in the fastest time
How to Play: Click cards to flip them and find matching pairs. When you match a pair, they stay flipped.
Strategy: Remember card positions, flip strategically. Pattern recognition is key!

SNAKE

Objective: Eat food to grow and avoid hitting walls or yourself
How to Play: Use arrow keys or WASD to move. Eat the red square (food) to grow longer.
Strategy: Plan ahead, avoid boxing yourself in. Use walls to create safe paths.

MINESWEEPER

Objective: Clear the grid without detonating any mines
How to Play: Left-click to reveal cells. Right-click to flag mines. Numbers show mine count nearby.
Difficulty Levels: Easy (8x8, 10 mines) | Medium (12x12, 30 mines) | Hard (16x16, 50 mines)

FLAPPY BIRD

Objective: Navigate through pipe obstacles and achieve the highest score
How to Play: Click or press SPACEBAR to flap. Pass through the gap between pipes.
Strategy: Timing is everything. Flap early and often to maintain altitude.

PONG

Objective: Beat the AI by scoring 5 points first
How to Play: Use W/S keys to move your paddle. Ball bounces off paddles and walls. Score when opponent misses.
Strategy: Position paddle at ball trajectory, control ball angle with paddle placement.

2048

Objective: Slide tiles to combine matching numbers and reach 2048
How to Play: Use arrow keys or WASD to move. When two tiles with the same number touch, they merge.
Strategy: Keep your highest tile in a corner, plan merges ahead, don't fill the grid haphazardly.

BREAKOUT

Objective: Destroy all bricks before the ball falls
How to Play: Move your mouse to control the paddle. Bounce the ball to hit bricks. Survive 3 lives.
Strategy: Lead the ball, position paddle for maximum rebound angles. Keep the ball in play!

HANGMAN

Objective: Guess the word before running out of attempts
How to Play: Click letters to guess. You have 6 wrong guesses allowed.
Strategy: Start with common letters (E, A, R, O), look for patterns, use context clues.
